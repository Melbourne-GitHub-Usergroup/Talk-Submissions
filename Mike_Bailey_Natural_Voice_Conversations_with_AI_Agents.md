# Natural Voice Conversations with AI Agents

## Speaker

**Mike Bailey** — Software Engineer and creator of [VoiceMode](https://github.com/mbailey/voicemode), an open-source tool that adds natural voice conversations to AI coding agents. Mike builds tools that make AI more accessible and human-friendly, with a focus on developer experience and open-source collaboration.

## Abstract

What if you could speak with your AI coding agent the way you would a colleague? [VoiceMode](https://github.com/mbailey/voicemode) is a free and open-source project that brings natural voice conversations to AI agents — turning text-based interactions into fluid, hands-free, screen-optional collaboration.

In this talk, I'll demonstrate live voice conversations with AI agents, show how voice changes the way developers interact with their tools, and explore what it means when your AI pair programmer can actually listen and respond naturally.

Voice also brings situational accessibility to developer tools — it's not just keyboard-optional, it's screen-optional. Walking the dog, vacuuming the house, or when a great idea strikes you in the shower — you can discuss it with your AI thinking partner, capture it, research it, and potentially have it implemented before you're even dressed. It's accessibility that applies to everyone, depending on their context.

Whether you use GitHub Copilot, Claude Code, or other AI-powered development tools, voice interaction opens up new possibilities for how we work with AI. This talk is tool-agnostic and focused on the developer experience of voice-first AI collaboration.

## Topics

- Live demo: natural voice conversations with AI coding agents
- How voice interaction changes the developer workflow
- Open-source tooling for voice-enabled AI
- The future of human-AI collaboration in software development
